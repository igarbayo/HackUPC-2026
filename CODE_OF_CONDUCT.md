# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the XEITECH project a welcoming
and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience
level, or identity.

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull
requests, code review comments, discussions, and any direct communication
among contributors.

---

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully — focus on the work, not the person.
- Acknowledge and value different viewpoints and areas of expertise.
- Help others learn and contribute, especially those who are new to the project.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

---

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not tolerated in any project space:

- Disrespectful, demeaning, or hostile language or behaviour.
- Personal attacks or ad hominem criticism.
- Sustained disruptive conduct after being asked to stop.
- Publishing private information about another person without consent.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider unprofessional or harmful.

---

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behaviour that violates this code of conduct,
please report it **privately by email**. Do not open a public issue.

Reports will be acknowledged within **48 hours**. All reports are kept
confidential. You may contact one maintainer or all of them simultaneously.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review each report and respond proportionally. Possible
responses include:

1. **Written reminder** — a private note explaining which behaviour was
   problematic and asking for it to stop.
2. **Temporary suspension** — a time-limited restriction on participating in
   project spaces.
3. **Permanent removal** — permanent exclusion from all project spaces for
   repeated or severe violations.

Decisions are made by the maintainers collectively and communicated privately
to the person concerned.

---

## Scope and precedence

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces (for
example, at a conference or in a published blog post).

In the event of ambiguity, the maintainers will use good judgement and
document the reasoning behind any enforcement decision.
